**Place names in Conducir.** The name of the street comes from the OSM field `name` in the road sections already loaded. `public/data/barrios.json` uses the 12 OSM reports from the coverage catalogue, without the margin applied to the layout. Polygons are in UTM 28N local meters, simplified to 25 cm and rounded to the nearest decimetre. `python scripts/data.py barrios` regenerates this file only. Attribution © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

**Acquisition controls.** In both areas the ArcGIS count matches the unique IDs downloaded, in pages ordered by 1,000 records. Preserved fields: OBJECTID and MAX_BLDGHEIGHT_1. The script repairs the geometries, intersects them with the area and discards fragments smaller than 2 m². The historical nucleus follows the envelope of the six original places with a 95 m margin. The next corridor uses the route in the catalog with a radius of 570 m and is intersected with the mainland bounded by the OSM coast. The cathedral uses a partition of the municipal building via the OSM contour to avoid overlapping two buildings.

**Heights.** The municipal field is labeled “Altura máxima”. The values ​​are interpreted as meters for the layout. Unit and precision are not certified by the layer metadata. Punctual checks: cathedral 41.5, Mercado 13.5, Theater 30.6, Gabinete 25.3. The maximum of a complex can overestimate the height of the eaves: the Gabinete has three levels in OSM. The scene distinguishes the provisional municipal values ​​from the estimate of 9 m for an unusable value. In the cathedral complex, the nave, attached volumes and towers with illustrative heights can be distinguished. The windows, domes, foliage and colors are procedural.

**THEN.** The OSM geometries are identified with way 30801430 (square), relation 19853960 (Casa de Colón, excluding three courtyards), way 259715457 (market), 251657540 (theater), 251672530 (Gabinete) and 584041194 (park). Each marker uses a point inside the polygon. For complete coordinates see [poi.geojson](../public/data/poi.geojson).
**Accuracy.** The terrain assumes local coincidence between the UTM 28N REGCAN95 grid and the WGS84 grid at raster resolution. The street axes have illustrated widths by class. Park colors can include paving. The route at Guiniguada follows current cartographic elements. The mesh is intended for urban visualization.

**Colors of the covers.** Colors derived from PNOA-MA orthophotography © Instituto Geográfico Nacional, conditions compatible with CC BY 4.0. The roof-colours.py script takes a 2,257 × 3,542 m mosaic from the [WMS PNOA-MA](https://www.ign.es/wms-inspire/pnoa-ma?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetCapabilities) and calculates a representative hue per footprint from the median of the halftones, excluding courtyards. The viewer uses the solid colors of `roof-tones.json`. Aerial source and polygons may refer to different dates; shadows, overhangs, and parallax affect sampling. The facades, crowns and stone of the towers have interpreted colours. The user preview and two clippings of the [ortofoto urbana GRAFCAN](https://www.idecanarias.es/listado_servicios/ortofoto-urbana-de-alta-resolucion-consolidado-final) led the visual comparison in Santa Ana, Mercado and San Telmo.

**Architectural details.** The additional volumes of Santa Ana (portico, bell towers, espadaña and dome) interpret the VT-524 card of the [municipal catalogue](https://www.idecanarias.es/resources/PLA_ENP_URB/URB_PLA/GC/LPGC/pep-vegtri/TNP/pepch-lpg-veguetatriana-dop-cp/pepch-lpg-veguetatriana-dop-cp05.pdf). The Gabinete elevation on the Plaza de Cairasco is an illustrative representation with galleries, clock and turrets. [Gabinete Literario architecture](https://gabineteliterario.com/arquitectura/). Detail dimensions are visual estimates.

**Arenales–Ciudad Jardín Extension.** Metric polygon area before rounding: 3,040,938 m². The four new references are Plaza de la Feria (way 30913318), El Obelisco (582805814), Parque Doramas (30801597) and Club de Natación Metropole (30801615). The ArcGIS acquisition rectangle contains 7,299 municipal records. The clipping contains 4,698 building parts, 1,433 road sections, 737 surfaces and 10,376 trees. The coordinates of the six original markers coincide with the previous revision.

**Sea.** `sea.json` comes from the `natural=coastline` streets of OpenStreetMap. The complete routes in the extracts, with an additional coastal cell to the west, separate the land from the ocean in a window with 2,800 m of margin around the perimeter of the model. The file contains water polygons, including the basins already represented, and the coast that guides seabed and foam. `python scripts/data.py sea` regenerates this file only. Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors.

**Control LiDAR (22 November 2026).** Tile `PNOA-2023-CANAR-459-3109-H28-NPC02.laz` (E 459–460 km, N 3108–3109 km, REGCAN95 / UTM 28N, 6.7 points/m², classification NPC02) from [Centro de Descargas del CNIG](https://centrodedescargas.cnig.es/CentroDescargas/lidar-tercera-cobertura). © Instituto Geográfico Nacional, CC BY 4.0 compatible license, attribution required. The file remains in `.cache/lidar` and does not enter Git. `scripts/lidar-check.py` compares municipal heights with those measured. `content/lidar-heights.json` only retains the LiDAR height (p95 of roof points minus the terrain median) of OBJECTIDs without valid municipal height; `data.py` uses it instead of the 9 m estimate and marks the building with `source: lidar-pnoa`. The rest is not changed.
