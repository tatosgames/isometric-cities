# Expand the layout

Start from the root of the repository. The procedure maintained along with the code is in [docs/EXPAND_CITY.md](../../../docs/EXPAND_CITY.md). See previously verified sources in [DATA_SOURCES.md](../../../docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) and desktop/mobile comparisons in [QA.md](../../../docs/QA.md).

- **Location inside the area.** Edit `content/las-palmas.json`. Retains published IDs even when the menu order changes. Check the internal point, the courtyards and any change in the perimeter before reusing the cache.
- **Neighborhood or pilot movement.** Add a zone to `coverage.zones`, with only one form: `osm` (barrio border relation), `municipal` (code `NUCL_CO_ID` of the municipal service «Barrios», with `name` in Spanish and accents; without `name` the zone is covered but not labeled), `places` with `margin`, `axis` with `radius` or `polygon`. Outside the main city (entity 24) indicate the entity with `entity`: Almatriche 1, Los Giles 12, Los Hoyos 14, Marzagán 19, San Lorenzo 30, Tamaraceite 36, Tenoya 37, La Montañeta 49, Tafira 50. Enlarge `coverage.bounds` until the coast crosses the window. Check that the union remains a single polygon. Details of the `municipal` zones are in [docs/EXPAND_CITY.md](../../../docs/EXPAND_CITY.md#borderless-neighborhoods-in-openstreetmap). Check the cut of the edge before measuring the surface. The cache distinguishes the parameters of each request.
- **Memory before each neighborhood.** The heap after the collection of `scripts/benchmark.mjs` (Chrome with phone emulation) and the one at the widest view on Schamann are the reference measure. Beyond approximately 100 MB the following neighborhood awaits a relief step ([QA](../../../docs/QA.md), [PERFORMANCE](../../../docs/PERFORMANCE.md)).
- **Monument or palette.** Start from the municipal OBJECTID and the catalog rules. Add dedicated geometry in `src/render/landmarks.ts` when the shape requires it. Use Google Maps for private visual consultation only. The published shades derive from PNOA.
- **Other city.** Catalog and output island. Adjust sources, projection, origin, initial view and attributions. Validate a pilot before expanding coverage.
- **Sectors.** Regenerate scenes, PNOA mosaic and then `python scripts/sectors.py`. Maintain common origin and grid, building ownership, texture edges and guidance graph independent of visual residence. Run `node scripts/qa-sectors.mjs` for WebGL traversals, memory, and leakage during an ongoing request.
- **Additional data (LiDAR, 20 cm orthophoto, other sources).** The catalog with links, licenses, reproducible access and results is in [docs/GEODATA_SOURCES.md](../../../docs/GEODATA_SOURCES.md). For buildings, foliage, roads and colours: PNOA LiDAR from CNIG (tile to be downloaded from the browser, never from script: 403 and reCAPTCHA) with `python scripts/lidar-check.py [--export]`, and 20 cm orthophoto from SITCAN read remotely (do not download the 10 GB) with `python -I scripts/roof-compare-20cm.py`. Do not use GRAFCAN WMS in the pipeline: it prohibits commercial use and massive dumping. Record each new source in the catalog and in `DATA_SOURCES.md` only when the data enters the map.
- **Dedicated objects and models.** Cranes and chimneys come from OpenStreetMap as `props` (`data.py`, `sectors.py`, `src/render/port-props.ts`); a landmark building has an entry in `catalog.buildings` and a function in `src/render/landmarks.ts`. Heights from LiDAR, plan from orthophoto at 20 cm, facades from non-reproduced reference photos. Procedure in [docs/EXPAND_CITY.md](../../../docs/EXPAND_CITY.md#dedicated-objects-and-models).
- **Optimization after each neighborhood.** Before moving on to the next neighborhood follow [docs/PERFORMANCE.md](../../../docs/PERFORMANCE.md): find the heaviest sectors, measure them with `node scripts/frame-profile.mjs <x,y,zoom>` at close range (1.59) and at maximum distance (0.352), count what makes up the triangles before touching the code and compare identical camera screenshots. Already active tricks that do not affect yield: simplified footprints at 0.1 m in `sectors.py`, extrusions without bottom and lid, single-sided parapet, similar neighbors merged in sectors from 240 buildings and up (`fused()`) and no shadow map in compact profile, no windows, frames and parapets buried in a taller neighbor, sector requests valid 600 ms, limited widest view to a neighborhood. Add to the catalog every new trick with its measure, and even the tried and tested ones that are not needed.
- **Deliver.** Perform the driving checks relevant to the modification. Record acquired data, approximations and measured results in local documents. Preserve identical camera comparison and verify WebGL recovery when geometry or textures grow.
